Congratulations to MSc project students Carolina Campos Quiroz, Isa Haque-Bousquet, Likai Shi, and Ziran Zhou.
This year's MSc projects addressed evolution of pseudoenzymes - functional proteins that are structurally related to enzymes, but whose modern-day function is different from the ancestral enzymatic function.
