Max Fairhurst is a Research Assistant expanding on the research conducting during his MScR project which focused on the role of Ssd1, an RNA-binding protein, on the abundance and localisation of cell wall proteins. 
Max also did his Honour's project in the Wallace Lab, focusing on the evolution of DNMT3, a DNA methyltransferase, within vertebrates.
