Domenico Modaffari was a PhD student working on the role of local translation during hyphal growth in filamentous fungi. He was a joint student with [Ken Sawin](https://sawin.bio.ed.ac.uk) at the [Wellcome Centre for Cell Biology](https://www.wcb.ed.ac.uk) and was funded by the [Wellcome Integrative Cell Mechanisms programme](https://www.wcb.ed.ac.uk/iCMPhD).

Domenico previously completed a BSc in Molecular Genetics at King's College London, and spent a year at the University of California, Berkeley during the course of his degree.

Domenico is currently a postdoctoral fellow in the group of [Samara Reck-Peterson](https://reckpetersonlab.org) at Weill Cornell College of Medicine in New York City.
